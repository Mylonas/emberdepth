# Privacy Policy for EmberDepth

_Last updated: 04 August 2026_

## The short version

EmberDepth does not ask for your name, your email, or any account. It does not
collect anything you type. Everything the game stores — your heroes, gear,
progress, and settings — stays on your phone.

The app shows optional rewarded ads and offers an in-app purchase to remove them.
The advertising network and the payment platform each collect some information,
described below.

## What the game itself stores

- Hero levels and formations
- Gear, materials, and forge state
- Prestige (Rebirth) progress and Ember upgrades
- Achievements, lifetime statistics, daily reward streak
- Tutorial progress and sound settings

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not
transmitted anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Advertising

EmberDepth shows optional rewarded ads supplied by **Google AdMob**. You choose
when to watch them; they are never forced. To serve ads, Google may collect and
process:

- your device's **advertising ID**
- general device information such as model, operating system version and language
- coarse, IP-derived location
- your interactions with the ads themselves

Google acts as an independent controller of that data. What it collects and how
it is used is described in:

- [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### Your choices

- **Reset or delete your advertising ID** — Android: Settings -> Privacy -> Ads.
- **Personalised ads** — if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app
  asks for your consent the first time it runs, and you can change that answer
  later from the privacy options in the same dialog.

## In-app purchases

EmberDepth offers a one-time purchase to remove ads, handled by **Google Play
Billing**. Google processes the transaction; the app never sees your payment
details. Your purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored on any
device where you sign in with the same account.

Google's handling of purchase data is covered by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

EmberDepth is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
any information from them.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — to fetch ads and verify purchases. The game itself works offline.
